Hi there,

I am Ridwan — Co-founder & CEO @ Pluto.

I’ve spent most of my career building 0→1 products & scaling businesses. First as a Data Platform Engineer at Shopify, where I helped scale Shopify’s petabyte-scale infra; then as a Product Lead at Dessa (acquired by Cash App), helping transform the company into a product-led business; and finally at Cash App.

At Cash App, I was a member of the product team. My team primarily focused on Cash App Pay and exploring new lines of business for Cash App through strategic development.

I studied Electrical & Computer Engineering at uWaterloo, where I was also part of the varsity Dragon Boat team.

But my career started early, before university, in Ad & Game Design — which is when I wrote my first line of code in C#, all to get pocket money to buy guitars.

People, Code & Design are the things I love the most.
