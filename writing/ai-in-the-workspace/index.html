AI in the workspace

Sep 2024

Image credits: the great designers at Perplexity

It’s Monday, September 9th, 2024, and I’ve noticed something strange about how I work now. I use Eraser.io AI sequence diagrams, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Cursor every day for more than 3 hours. It feels like my whole way of working changed almost overnight, and I can’t really remember how I worked 1.5 years ago.

Was I really starting from scratch for every PRD, every bit of market research, or every blog post I wrote? That seems weird now. The idea of doing all that without AI feels strange. Today, it feels kind of odd that anyone would start from scratch.

My calories are now tracked in ChatGPT. Holiday planning? GPT. Shell script? WordPress script? GPT. AppScript to automate how I work with Google Sheets? GPT. Forgot how to easily do pivot tables — GPT? Birthday card for a friend? GPT!

The fact that I can’t remember what it felt like to do everything manually probably means we’re getting used to this new way of working.

Weird…
